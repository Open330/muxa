# Agent logos

`Assets.xcassets/AgentLogos` holds the official marks of the agent products
Tend can run or ask. Every mark is a trademark of its owner. Tend uses them
only to identify the product next to (or in place of) its name. Their use
does not mean the owner endorses or sponsors Tend.

The marks have not been redrawn or altered. Files were only resized or
cropped to the mark: a viewBox tightened around the artwork, or a
transparent margin trimmed. Where a vendor file put the mark on a
background disc, the disc was left out. Each `-mono` variant has the same
outline filled with one colour and is rendered as a template, for the menu
bar's white-on-colour faces.

All files were fetched on 2026-11-12.

Codex appears as the OpenAI blossom because Codex is an OpenAI product.
openai.com/brand refused automated fetches (HTTP 403), so the blossom comes
from OpenAI's own developer site.

Brand notes:

- OpenCode's brand page: "OpenCode and the OpenCode logo are trademarks of
  Anomaly. Do not use them to suggest that Anomaly endorses or sponsors you."
  Use the light version on light backgrounds and the dark version on dark
  ones. The asset catalog switches between them by appearance.
- Anthropic's press kit supplies the Claude spark and Anthropic symbol for
  media and partner use.
- Tend never recolours a mark that has brand colours, and never draws a tint
  behind one.
